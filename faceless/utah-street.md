# Faceless B-roll #1 (Step 4a): establishing shot for a salisburyre footage cut. No people, no text, no plates.
Photoreal vertical 9:16 drone-style establishing shot of a quiet new-construction residential street in Utah County,
Utah at golden hour: two-story modern-farmhouse homes with white board-and-batten siding and black window frames,
fresh sod and young trees, a few driveways still bare concrete, the Wasatch mountains warm and soft in the distance.
Slow, steady push-in along the street at eye-to-rooftop height, gentle parallax, long shadows, no people, no cars
moving, no yard signs, no house numbers, no logos, no text of any kind. Clean documentary grade, natural color.
